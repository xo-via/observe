## Overview

Not every account on XO is the same. Understanding where someone sits in the lifecycle helps us tailor messaging, support, and product decisions.

XO operates in two distinct spaces — **Community** and **B2B** — and each has its own set of profile categories. 

We use deliberately different terminology between the two to maintain clear distinction and avoid confusion internally.

<Callout title="NOTE" type="Notes">
These Terminologies are created internally & will be used to throughout the docs to distinguish between different personas experimenting with beta Platform.
</Callout>

### Community Edition Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Sign-up** | Someone who has created an XO account but is not actively using the platform. They registered but haven't started a trial or launched a workspace. |
| **User** | Someone who has started a trial and is actively using workspaces. They are evaluating XO and engaging with the core product. |
| **Customer** | Anyone on a paid Community plan. The moment an individual commits financially, they become a Customer. |

### B2B Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Prospect** | A business that is exploring and demoing with XO. They are in early-stage evaluation with no commercial commitment yet. |
| **High-Value Prospect** | A business with a confirmed budget of $10k or more that is actively evaluating XO. These warrant dedicated, consultative attention. |
| **Client** | A business on a paid XO plan. This is the B2B equivalent of a Customer — the term is intentionally different to avoid confusion across the two spaces. |

## Terminology Quick Reference

To keep internal communication clean:

- When talking about the **community/self-serve side**, use: Sign-up, User, Customer.
- When talking about the **B2B/sales-led side**, use: Prospect, High-Value Prospect, Client.
- Never mix the two. If someone says "customer," they mean an individual on a paid community plan. If they mean a paying business, the word is "client."
