## Resale Partnership Overview

EVM holds a resale license for XO's AI workspace platform, positioning them as a market-focused extension of XO's distribution and customer success strategy. EVM is not a technology vendor competing with XO — they're a strategic go-to-market partner bringing their own customer relationships, industry expertise, and sales channels.

The model allows EVM to own customer relationships (clients contract with EVM, not XO), control commercial terms (pricing, packaging, billing), customize the platform (branding, governance), and build customer-specific governance (RBAC, approval workflows, agent templates).

## Customization Capabilities

### Dashboard and Branding
Custom logo, color schemes, and visual identity throughout the XO interface. White-labeled domain naming (e.g., `clients.evm.com`). Custom navigation, help text, and onboarding flows.

### Governance and Access Control
Role-based access control configuration per client organizational structure. Custom approval workflows for agent deployments, skill deployments, and workspace provisioning. User management integration with client identity systems.

### Agent Templates and Presets
Pre-configured agent templates tuned to client industry or use case. Custom skills and MCP integrations specific to client workflows. Agent library organization and categorization per client requirements.

### Support Tiers
Multiple support levels (basic, standard, premium) with SLA guarantees. EVM provides Tier-1 support directly to clients; XO backs Tier-2 escalation. Custom SLA agreements and support channel preferences per client.

## Commercial Model

**Billing flow:** EVM clients pay EVM directly. EVM pays XO a resale fee based on negotiated commercial terms. This decouples XO's revenue from client relationship management.

**Support escalation:** EVM supports their clients directly. For platform-level issues, security concerns, or feature requests requiring XO platform changes, EVM escalates to XO's support and engineering teams.

## Relationship with XO

XO provides the platform (infrastructure, APIs, core agents, MCP support, ongoing development) and escalation support. EVM provides market coverage (business development, customer success, domain expertise) and Tier-1 support (onboarding, custom configuration, troubleshooting).
