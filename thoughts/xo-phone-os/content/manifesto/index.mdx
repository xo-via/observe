# The XO Manifesto

We believe every person and every team deserves an army of competent
digital workers. Not a chatbot. Not a tool. **A workspace.**

## What we build

XO is the operating layer for AI agents. Two pieces, one promise:

1. **The Coworker** is the workspace. Runtime, memory, files, tools,
   and chat surface in a single portable image. Runs on a laptop, in
   a sandbox, on Coder, on Vercel, anywhere a container can land.
2. **The Swarm** is the platform. Launches and manages many Coworker
   workspaces. Identity, billing, observability, templates, and the
   shared catalog of integrations that make every agent useful.

A Coworker without the Swarm is a powerful local tool. A Swarm
without Coworkers is just another control plane. Stacked together
they're the smallest unit of an autonomous digital agency.

## What we believe

**Agents should have their own workspaces.** A real desk: persistent
memory, file system, secrets, runtime. Not a stateless API call.

**Agents should be portable.** The same workspace image runs on your
laptop, on a remote sandbox, on a cloud container. The user owns
where work happens.

**Agents should be composable.** Tools, integrations, and skills are
modular. Every workspace can specialize without forking the platform.

**Agents should be trustworthy.** Open runtime, inspectable state,
clear permissions. Trust is earned by being legible, not by closing
the door.

**Agents should serve their humans.** The point of the workspace is
the human in front of it. Speed, clarity, and answerability beat
"impressive autonomy" every time.

## What we are not

We are not a chatbot vendor. We are not an automation script. We are
not a single-model wrapper. We are the workspace where any agent
runs any tool against any model on behalf of any user.

## Where we go

To the day when every person, team, and small business has the same
operating leverage that today only the largest companies can afford.
Agents that work. Workspaces that travel. Tools that connect.

That's XO.
