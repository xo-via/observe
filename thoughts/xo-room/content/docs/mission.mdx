# Mission

<Callout>
  **XO is the infrastructure layer that lets anyone deploy, manage, and scale AI agents — empowering everyone to have their own Agent Workforce.**
</Callout>

## What XO exists to do

Make AI agents productive from the moment they're created. Today, deploying an AI agent in any real way takes weeks of DevOps work per agent. XO removes that wall.

<DeploymentComparisonDiagram title="From weeks of DevOps to one click" />

Our work is the layer underneath every coding agent, every research agent, every messaging-channel agent, every custom agent. We don't build the agents. We make sure they can run.

## The world we're building toward

A world where every person and every business has an **Agent Workforce running for them.** Not one chatbot. Not a solo coding assistant — a coordinated team of agents, specialized, always-on, doing real work.

<AgentWorkforceDiagram title="Your personal Agent Workforce" />

In that world, deploying an agent is as simple as picking one. The infrastructure is invisible. The agentic experience is unparalleled.

## What is our edge

Our edge is that we are not only building a place to run agents — we are building the coordination layer they need once agents become a real part of daily work. **The agentic economy is on the rise.** XO is positioned to lead it.

<AgenticEconomyDiagram title="The agentic economy is on the rise — XO is the pioneer" />

As AI systems mature, agents will not just sit inside one app. They will move context across workspaces, devices, teams, tools, and organizations while preserving identity, permissions, and trust. That creates a new infrastructure problem: **how do agents securely talk to each other and coordinate work?**

XO is investing early in agent-to-agent communication. If we get this right, XO becomes more than a deployment surface — it becomes the trusted network where agents discover each other, exchange state, and hand off work safely.

<Callout>
  **The long-term platform is not just agent hosting. It is the communication and coordination network for agentic organizations.**
</Callout>

## What stays constant

Surfaces change. Dashboards evolve. Templates expand. The infrastructure mandate is constant: **the agentic experience XO provides is unparalleled.** Every decision widens that gap. That's the product. That's the moat.
